The beginning · JULY 2023

When our story began

Who would've thought that a regular day at COCOLIFE would turn into the beginning of our favorite love story? It was July 2023. I was already a regular employee, just minding my own business, when you came along as the new hire. At first, we were just two coworkers who happened to cross paths. We eventually became friends… then professional annoyances to each other… and somehow, without either of us noticing, we became something more. 😂 We had our little circle of friends at work, and everyone probably thought we were just two people who loved teasing, annoying, and messing with each other. Little did they know, behind all those playful arguments and endless kulitan, something beautiful was quietly growing. And the funniest part? We kept our relationship a secret for months! We managed to keep our little love story under wraps until I finally resigned from COCOLIFE. Only then did our friends discover that the two people they thought were just busy annoying each other were actually falling in love all along. HAHAHA! Who would've guessed that my workplace would introduce me to my favorite person? From coworkers, to friends, to enemies (well, at least we pretended to be 😝), and finally to lovers. I guess the best things really do come unexpectedly. And among all the people I could have met at work, I'm glad it was you, Love. Plot twist of the year: I went to work, but somehow ended up finding my forever person. ❤️

A favorite memory

The moment I knew

You know what's funny, Love? I didn't even realize I was falling for you until I noticed that talking to you had slowly become my favorite part of the day. Back then, we were just friends. We'd spend our time at work teasing each other, exchanging jokes, and finding every possible reason to annoy one another. But once working hours were over, that was it. No more kulitan, no more conversations. We had our own lives outside work. Work is work, kumbaga. But somewhere along the way, things started to change. I started finding reasons to message you even after work. Yung tipong, buong araw na kitang kinukulit sa office, pero pag-uwi, ikaw pa rin ang gusto kong kausap. HAHAHA! 😂 What used to be conversations that ended after office hours slowly turned into late-night talks. And before I knew it, I was already looking forward to talking to you every single night. And here's my favorite part... Dati, before 8 PM, tulog ka na. As in, certified early sleeper ka talaga! HAHAHA! But because of my endless kakulitan, somehow, I managed to turn you into a puyat girlie. 😂❤️ Imagine that! From someone who used to sleep before 8 PM to someone who would stay up late just to entertain my nonsense. Sorry na agad, Love, nasira ko sleeping schedule mo. HAHAHA! But behind all those jokes, teasing, and sleepless nights, something was happening that I didn't immediately understand. I was slowly falling for you. I realized that I wasn't just messaging you because I was bored or because I wanted someone to tease. I was messaging you because I genuinely wanted to be part of your day, even after our workday had ended. Your replies became something I looked forward to. Our conversations became something I didn't want to end. And somehow, the girl I used to annoy just for fun became the girl I couldn't go a day without talking to. That's when I knew. It wasn't some grand romantic moment. There were no fireworks, no dramatic background music, and definitely no slow-motion movie scene. It was just me, staring at my phone, smiling at our conversations, and realizing... "Ay, hala. Parang gusto ko na 'to ah." 😭❤️ And looking back, Love, I think that's what makes our story so special. I didn't fall for you all at once. I fell for you through the simplest things: our random conversations, our silly arguments, our endless kulitan, and those nights when neither of us wanted to say goodnight just yet. I guess I didn't just steal your sleep, Love. Somewhere along the way, you stole my heart, too. 🌷❤️

Today & always

Still choosing you

Love, I want you to remember something, especially on the days when things aren't easy between us. No matter how many challenges we face, no matter how many misunderstandings, doubts, or difficult days come our way, I hope you never forget that you are still the person I choose. And I will keep choosing you, even on the days when loving each other takes a little more effort. I know sometimes you get bothered by the way my mind works. I know I overthink, have doubts, and sometimes make things more complicated than they need to be. I'm not perfect, and I know I still have a lot to work on. But please know that I'm trying. I'm trying to understand myself better, to become a better partner, and to be someone you can feel secure with. But Love, I hope you can help me along the way, too. Please don't always make me feel like I have to fight against you just to make things right. Wag mo naman sana akong laging i-challenge. HAHAHA! I know we won't always agree, and that's okay. We can have different opinions, we can get annoyed with each other, and we can have our little tampuhan moments. But at the end of the day, I hope we remember that we're on the same team. I'm not just trying to win in life for myself anymore. I'm trying my best to build a life where we can both win, grow, and enjoy everything we've worked so hard for. I want to celebrate your achievements, support you through your struggles, and experience all the beautiful things life has waiting for us. So please, Love, don't be my opponent when life itself is already challenging enough. Be my teammate. Let's not fight against each other when we're supposed to be fighting for the same dreams. When things get difficult, let's talk. When one of us gets tired, let's learn to understand. When doubts come in, let's reassure each other instead of letting them pull us apart. I don't expect us to have a perfect love story. I just want us to have one where we never forget why we started, where we learn from our mistakes, and where we continue choosing each other despite everything. Kampi tayo dito, Love. It's you and me against the problems, not you against me. I'm still learning, still growing, and still figuring things out. But one thing I don't want you to question is this: Out of all the people I could walk through life with, I want it to be you. Today, tomorrow, on our easy days, and even on our difficult ones. Still you. Still us. Still choosing you. ❤️
